## DATA QUALITY & LIMITATIONS NOTES

### Sources Cited Responsibly

- **Government Data (Most Reliable):** PIB, Ministry of Agriculture, World Bank, NSSO
  - Data freshness: 2024-2026 for most metrics
  - Methodology: Official statistical surveys (ADIS, PLFS, DGCES)

- **Market Research (Projections):** IMARC, Mordor, Ken Research, Tracxn
  - Clearly marked as projections; derived from trend analysis
  - Confidence varies by metric (historical > near-future > long-term)

- **Academic/Peer-Reviewed:** ResearchGate, ScienceDirect, Springer, SAGE
  - Published 2024-2025; recent research
  - Reflects state-of-knowledge on adoption barriers, climate impacts

- **News/Analysis:** Inc42, Your Story, Farmonaut, Down to Earth
  - Industry coverage; startup/technology updates
  - Less authoritative for macro stats, but good for case studies

### Known Limitations

1. **Rural Data Gaps:** Rural income data primarily from 2019 ADIS; newer surveys in progress
2. **Startup Data:** Funding databases (Tracxn) may miss informal funding rounds
3. **Climate Vulnerability:** NICRA assessed 651 districts; full national coverage ongoing
4. **Digital Literacy:** Estimates vary 25-35%; precise baseline unclear
5. **AI Adoption Rates:** Global data (70M acres, 58% adoption) from 2024; India breakdown estimates

## HOW TO USE THIS DOCUMENT

For formal report writing:

1. **Find stat/claim** → Look up in this document by topic
2. **Cite original source** → Use the URL and author/organization provided
3. **Note date** → Include publication/data year for credibility
4. **Add context** → Where applicable, note data source (e.g., government survey, research projection)

Example in-text citation:
> "India's agriculture contributed ₹53.85 lakh crore to GVA in FY 2024-25 (Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, December 2025), representing 16-17.6% of national GDP and employing approximately 150 million workers."
