## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

India's agriculture sector contributes approximately **16-17.6% to GDP** and employs **~46% of the population (~150 million workers)**. The sector is experiencing transformative growth through AI and digital technologies, with the agritech market projected to expand from **$9 billion (2025) to $28 billion by 2030** at a **25% CAGR**. However, critical gaps exist in farmer adoption, digital infrastructure, and affordability for smallholder farmers.

| Metric | 2024 Value | Change |
|--------|-----------|--------|

- Median farmer household income is extremely low (~USD 1,470/year), insufficient for family needs

- High income volatility (30% experience crop failures, 12% suffer price losses)
- Informal credit keeps farmers in debt cycles despite policy push toward banking

| Challenge | Impact | Scale |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| High Solution Costs | Out of reach for smallholders | Majority of 142M smallholder farmers |
| Device Costs | Smartphones, sensors expensive | Rural purchasing power limited |
