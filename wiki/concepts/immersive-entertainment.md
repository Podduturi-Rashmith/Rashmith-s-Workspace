# Immersive Entertainment

A category of entertainment in which the audience is physically or perceptually placed *inside* the experience — rather than watching it from a distance — through location-based environments, participatory storytelling, sensory engagement, and responsive design.

---

## Explanation

Immersive entertainment encompasses: location-based experiences (Meow Wolf, teamLab), immersive theatre (Punchdrunk's *Sleep No More*), IP-driven experiential venues (Netflix House), XR/VR experiences, and interactive narrative environments. The defining characteristic is that the audience is an active participant rather than a passive viewer. You don't watch the story — you move through it.

[[charles-melcher]] calls these **"living stories"** — experiences that are agentic, immersive, embodied, responsive, social, and transformative.

---

## Why It Matters

This is the emerging answer to [[franchise-fatigue]], [[ai-slop]] saturation, and declining passive viewership in both cinema and sports. Every major consultancy (EY, McKinsey, Deloitte, PwC) and major operator (Netflix, Disney, the NBA) is converging on immersive and experiential formats as the next growth engine in entertainment.

The theoretical foundation is [[experience-economy]] — B. Joseph Pine II and James H. Gilmore's thesis that experiences are the next layer of economic value above goods and services.

## Real-World Examples

- **Netflix House** — Netflix's permanent 100,000+ sq ft experiential venues (Philadelphia 2024, Dallas/King of Prussia 2025, Las Vegas 2027)
- **Punchdrunk's Sleep No More** — immersive theatre, 9 years running in NYC; Shanghai production generated $75 million
- **teamLab** — digital art immersive spaces; 4.2 million visitors to Tokyo museums in 2025
- **Meow Wolf** — surreal experiential art installations across the US
- **The Bridgerton Experience** — IP-driven immersive event, 11 cities, 150,000+ visitors quickly
- **MSG Sphere, Las Vegas** — 160,000 sq ft wraparound LED, haptic seats, beamforming audio

## The Gen Z Signal

McKinsey (January 2026): Gen Z is **4x more likely than older generations** to attend an immersive experience — the same generation most disengaged from passive TV and cinema. They haven't abandoned entertainment; they've abandoned *watching.*

52% of people now attend 6+ immersive experiences per year. 73% will travel specifically for one.
