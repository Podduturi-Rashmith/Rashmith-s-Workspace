# Entertainment

Domain covering the movies and sports industries — their current state, audience trends, future trajectories, and the emerging immersive/experiential formats replacing passive screen-based entertainment.

---

## Overview

This domain tracks everything related to how audiences consume movies and sports, how those industries are changing under pressure from streaming, AI, short-form video, and generational shifts, and where the next generation of entertainment experience is heading.

The central insight emerging from current sources: **passive entertainment is structurally declining. Immersive, live, participatory, and experiential entertainment is the growth frontier.**

---

## Current Understanding

### The Dying Model
- Cinema attendance in the US is at ~61% of pre-pandemic levels and may not fully recover
- Hollywood is trapped in a [[franchise-fatigue]] spiral — more sequels, lower returns
- Traditional sports broadcast is losing Gen Z — only 23% identify as passionate fans
- [[ai-slop]] is accelerating audience rejection of formulaic, inauthentic content

### The Growing Exception
- **India** is the outlier — cinema attendance is *rising*, IPL cricket reaches 620 million viewers, and the appetite for live entertainment is expanding
- **IPL** set the world record for live streaming concurrent viewers (32 million) in 2023
- Gen Z **is** engaged — just differently: highlights, social content, athlete stories, fantasy sports, immersive experiences

### The Emerging Model
- [[immersive-entertainment]]: $137B market growing to $1 trillion by 2033
- Netflix House, teamLab, Sleep No More, Meow Wolf — physical, participatory experiences
- The [[experience-economy]] thesis: experiences → transformations as the premium economic layer
- [[creator-economy]]: $1.35 trillion by 2035; creators as the authentic alternative to AI slop and studio product
