# Andrej Karpathy

AI researcher and educator; originator of the [[llm-wiki-pattern]] for building LLM-maintained personal knowledge bases.

---

## Overview

Andrej Karpathy is a prominent AI researcher known for his work at OpenAI and Tesla, and for widely-followed educational content on deep learning. In this wiki he is significant as the author of the "LLM Wiki" concept document — a pattern for using LLMs to incrementally build and maintain a persistent, interlinked markdown wiki over raw source documents. This document is the direct ancestor of the schema (`CLAUDE.md`) governing this vault.

---

## What the Wiki Knows

Karpathy's LLM Wiki document articulates a three-layer architecture: immutable raw sources, an LLM-owned wiki layer, and a schema file that tells the LLM how to behave. His key insight is that the bottleneck in personal knowledge management is not reading or thinking — it's bookkeeping. LLMs eliminate that cost entirely, allowing the wiki to compound indefinitely without human maintenance.

The document was popularised by [[defileo]] in an April 2026 X thread, which embedded the full Karpathy prompt as the "most important step" in setting up a Claude + Obsidian second brain.
