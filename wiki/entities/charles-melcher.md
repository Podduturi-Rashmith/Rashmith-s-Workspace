# Charles Melcher

Founder of the Future of Storytelling Summit and author of *The Future of Storytelling: How Immersive Experiences Are Transforming Our World* (Artisan Books, 2025); the clearest articulator of the "end of passive entertainment" thesis.

---

## Overview

Charles Melcher founded the Future of Storytelling (FoST) Summit, an annual gathering exploring how emerging technologies are reshaping narrative and human experience. His 2025 book is the most comprehensive survey of [[immersive-entertainment]] published to date, covering 50+ real-world examples and arguing that "living stories" — participatory, embodied, responsive experiences — are the successor to passive film and television.

---

## Core Thesis

> *"These days we don't want to just watch our media — we want to be a part of it. To touch, feel, and test our surroundings, to make decisions that directly affect the story's outcome. Immersive events are growing at such a staggering rate that their revenue will soon outpace film and television combined."*

He defines the new format as **"living stories"** — experiences that are:
- **Agentic** — the audience has agency
- **Immersive** — physically surrounding
- **Embodied** — felt in the body, not just seen
- **Responsive** — reacts to the participant
- **Social** — shared with others
- **Transformative** — changes the participant

---

## What the Wiki Knows

Melcher draws on [[joseph-pine]]'s [[experience-economy]] framework throughout his book. He is a featured voice at the Future of Storytelling Summit alongside Pine himself. Angela Ahrendts (former Apple SVP, former Burberry CEO) wrote of his work: *"Immersive storytelling isn't just about entertainment — it's the future of engagement, education, brand loyalty, and human connection."*
