# Defileo (Leo)

X/Twitter creator and practitioner who popularised the [[llm-wiki-pattern]] as a personal "second brain" setup using [[claude-code]] and [[obsidian]].

---

## Overview

Leo (@defileo on X) is a creator focused on productivity, AI tools, and personal knowledge management. He is best known in this wiki for a viral April 2026 thread that distilled [[andrej-karpathy]]'s LLM Wiki concept into a practical, step-by-step setup guide accessible to a general audience. His framing — "Claude + Obsidian should be illegal" — became the hook for a wide popularisation of the pattern.

---

## What the Wiki Knows

Leo's contribution is primarily as a practitioner-communicator: he takes the abstract [[llm-wiki-pattern]] architecture and translates it into copy-paste CLI prompts, a concrete setup sequence, and motivational framing around why most [[second-brain]] systems fail and how LLM-maintained vaults solve that. He explicitly credits [[andrej-karpathy]] as the originator of the schema/prompt.

His practical additions beyond Karpathy's original include:
- A `Memory.md` pattern for bootstrapping Claude's context about the user
- The [[morning-digest-automation]] cron script concept
- Prompts for processing call transcripts and tracking action items
