# B. Joseph Pine II

Business strategist and co-author of *The Experience Economy* (1998/2020) with James H. Gilmore; originator of the theoretical framework underpinning the modern [[immersive-entertainment]] revolution.

## What the Wiki Knows

Pine and Gilmore's core thesis — that experiences are the next premium economic layer above goods and services, and that transformations are the layer above experiences — has become the dominant framework for understanding the shift from passive to immersive entertainment in 2025–2026.

Their 1998 HBR article predates Netflix, smartphones, and TikTok. Yet it precisely describes what every major entertainment company and consultancy is now doing:
- Netflix House = staging experiences around IP
- teamLab = selling transformative encounters with digital art
- Sleep No More = participatory theatre as premium experience
- IPL free streaming = making the experience accessible to expand the transformation

Pine is a featured voice at the Future of Storytelling Summit, alongside [[charles-melcher]] who cites him throughout *The Future of Storytelling*.
