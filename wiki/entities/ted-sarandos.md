# Ted Sarandos

Co-CEO of Netflix; the most prominent executive voice arguing that the traditional movie theatre model is obsolete and that the future of entertainment is physical experience venues and streaming.

## What the Wiki Knows

Sarandos declared at the Time100 Summit (April 2025) that the movie theatre model is *"an outmoded idea — for most people, not for everybody."* He argued Hollywood must stop being trapped by wanting audiences to see films theatrically "because that's how the film industry wants audiences to watch them."

Paradoxically, Sarandos is simultaneously the CEO overseeing **Netflix House** — Netflix's chain of permanent physical experiential venues (Philadelphia 2024, Dallas/King of Prussia 2025, Las Vegas 2027). He describes these as *"the next generation of what a theme park might be"* — venues people visit 4–5 times per year rather than once every 5 years.

This is not a contradiction. The thesis is: the passive cinema screen is dead, but physical experiences built around IP are the growth opportunity. Netflix is moving from showing content to staging experiences — a direct operationalisation of the [[experience-economy]].
