# Vannevar Bush

American engineer and science administrator; author of the 1945 essay "As We May Think," in which he described the [[memex]] — a hypothetical personal knowledge machine and the spiritual ancestor of the [[llm-wiki-pattern]].

---

## Overview

Vannevar Bush (1890–1974) served as Director of the Office of Scientific Research and Development during WWII and was one of the most influential figures in 20th-century American science policy. His 1945 Atlantic essay "As We May Think" is most relevant here: in it he proposed the Memex — a personal desk-sized device for storing, retrieving, and linking documents via "associative trails." The idea anticipates hypertext, personal computing, and modern PKM systems.

---

## What the Wiki Knows

Bush appears in this wiki as a historical reference point invoked by both [[andrej-karpathy]] and [[defileo]]. Both cite him to argue that the [[llm-wiki-pattern]] fulfils a vision Bush articulated 80 years ago — a private, curated knowledge store where the *connections between documents* are as valuable as the documents themselves. The part Bush couldn't solve: who does the maintenance. Their answer: the LLM.
