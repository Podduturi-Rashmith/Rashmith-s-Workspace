## Domains Covered

### PKM (Personal Knowledge Management)
The [[llm-wiki-pattern]] — raw sources + LLM-owned wiki + schema — is the architecture this vault implements. Originated by [[andrej-karpathy]], popularised by [[defileo]]. The core insight: LLMs eliminate the maintenance burden that causes all human-maintained knowledge systems to decay. This vault is itself a live demonstration of that thesis.

### Entertainment
Two research queries have built out a rich picture of where movies and sports are heading. The central finding: passive screen-based entertainment is structurally declining; immersive, participatory, and live experiences are the growth frontier. India is the key outlier — cinema attendance is rising, IPL is breaking streaming records, and the appetite for live entertainment is expanding.

## Major Themes Across the Vault

1. **Compounding systems beat point-in-time tools.** True in PKM (wiki vs. RAG) and in entertainment (experiences vs. disposable content). The pattern is the same: build something that accumulates value over time rather than being consumed and forgotten.

2. **Authenticity becomes scarce and premium as AI saturates everything.** [[ai-slop]] is measurable and growing. The [[creator-economy]] and [[immersive-entertainment]] are both responses to the same underlying problem: algorithmically-generated sameness.

3. **India is the underserved opportunity.** The only cinema market growing. The world's largest cricket audience. Gen Z that wants new experiences. Almost no one building the next-generation entertainment infrastructure for that market.

4. **The progression: Radio → TV → Experience.** [[vannevar-bush]]'s [[memex]] (1945) anticipated the PKM revolution. [[joseph-pine]]'s [[experience-economy]] (1998) anticipated the entertainment revolution. Both theses are arriving simultaneously.
