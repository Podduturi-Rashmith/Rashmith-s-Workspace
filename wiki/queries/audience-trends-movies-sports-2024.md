# Audience Trends: Movies and Sports (2019–2024)

*Research compiled in response to: "Do research on audience interest going up or down in the movie industry and sports industry."*

---

## Movie Industry — Is Audience Interest Going Up or Down?

**Short answer: Down in attendance, partially recovered in revenue, structurally threatened by streaming.**

### Global Box Office Revenue

| Year | Revenue | vs. 2019 Peak |
|------|---------|--------------|
| 2019 | ~$42 billion | 100% (peak) |
| 2020 | Collapsed ~70% | ~30% |
| 2021 | ~$21.3 billion | 51% |
| 2022 | ~$25.9 billion | 62% |
| 2023 | ~$33.2 billion | ~80% |
| 2024 | ~$32.5–33 billion | ~78% |
| 2029 (forecast) | $41.5 billion | ~99% |

*Sources: Statista, PwC Global E&M Outlook 2024–28, WIPO Global Innovation Index Blog*

### Cinema Attendance: Still Deeply Below Pre-Pandemic Levels

- US attendance in 2023 was at approximately **61% of 2019 levels** — 4 in 10 pre-pandemic cinema-goers have not returned
- Revenue looks better than attendance because ticket prices have risen significantly
- PwC: global cinema admissions will not rebound even by 2027
- Key barriers: cost (52% cite ticket prices), streaming convenience, habit shifts from lockdowns

### Streaming Has Won — For Now

- Streaming crossed **40% of all US TV usage** in June 2024 — an all-time record (Nielsen)
- Linear TV fell **below 50% of total US usage** for the first time in July 2023
- Netflix ended 2024 with **301.6 million subscribers** — adding 41.3 million in the year
- Global OTT subscriptions: 1.6 billion (2023) → projected 2.1 billion by 2028
- Global streaming market: $129 billion (2024) → projected $417 billion by 2030

### Gen Z Signal — The Danger Warning

- **47% of Gen Z** prefer social video and live streams over movies
- **Only 11%** prefer movies from streaming services
- Gen Z is increasingly choosing creator-driven content over big-budget entertainment (Deloitte)
- YouTube Shorts reaches more 18–19-year-olds than any television network globally
- **74% of Gen Z** prefer original content over franchises/remakes (Tubi, 2024)

### India — The Exception to Every Rule

**The only surveyed market where frequent cinema-going increased** — from 41% in 2019 to **49% in 2023** (S&P Global). South Indian cinema generated 52 billion rupees in 2023. Bollywood record-breakers (Jawan, Pathaan) drove record domestic numbers. Asia-Pacific now represents **39% of global box office**, the leading regional share.

## Sports Industry — Is Audience Interest Going Up or Down?

**Short answer: Major leagues are booming. But Gen Z is the structural crack.**

### Major League Viewership — Hitting Records

**IPL Cricket:**
- 2023: ~505 million viewers; JioCinema free streaming drove the 2023 final to **32 million concurrent viewers** — world record for live streaming
- 2024: **620 million average viewers**; **350 billion minutes** streamed digitally
- Key driver: JioCinema made IPL free on digital — barrier to access collapsed

**NFL:**
- 2024 season: **18.6 million viewers per game** — 9-year high
- 2025 season: **18.7 million per game** — second-highest since 1988
- Amazon Prime Thursday Night Football grew **60%** from 2022 to 2025

**Premier League:**
- Broadcast in 189 countries; ~1.87 billion global weekly followers
- 2023–24: **7% global audience increase** — China +373%, USA +42%
- First Premier League match to exceed 2 million US viewers: Man City vs Arsenal, March 2024

**T20 Cricket Globally:**
- Leagues now in **23 countries across 6 continents**
- 2024 ICC T20 World Cup: **9 billion video views** — up 158% from previous record
- Format expanded to 20 teams including USA debut

### Gen Z — The Structural Crack

| Metric | Gen Z | Other Generations |
|--------|-------|------------------|
| Passionate sports fans | 23% | 42% (Millennials) |
| "Anti-sports" | 27% | Single digits |
| Don't watch sports at all | 33% | 24% |
| Never attended a professional event | ~50% | Much lower |

- **34% of Gen Z** prefer highlights over full games
- BUT: 63% say athlete social media increases engagement; 54% engage via fantasy sports
- They watch live sports at home with others **61% of the time** — social context matters more than broadcast

## The Common Thread — Both Industries Fighting the Same Enemy

Both industries are losing the attention war to the same competitors simultaneously:

| Competitor | Impact |
|-----------|--------|
| TikTok / YouTube Shorts | 70 billion daily Shorts views; reaches more 18–19 year olds than any TV network |
| Live streaming | 32.5 billion hours in 2024 — double 2019 levels |
| Gaming | Competes with both; paradoxically *increases* sports engagement via fantasy/gaming tie-ins |
| Creator content | 56% of Gen Z say it feels "more relevant" than traditional TV or movies |

### The Winning Strategy for Both Industries

Both cinema and sports are converging on the same answer: **lean into live, premium, social, and shorter-format experiences that cannot be replicated by an algorithm.**

- Sports: T20 cricket, NFL RedZone, highlight culture, athlete social content
- Cinema: India's theatrical culture, event films, premium large format
- Both: Live experiences as the one thing streaming genuinely cannot replace
